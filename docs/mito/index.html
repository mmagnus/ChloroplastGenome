PEP promoters and sigma factors

Promoters are RpoB ptChIP peaks in 4-day Col-0 seedlings (log2 ≥ 1). SIG2 dependence is the drop in polymerase binding in the sig2 mutant after removing the genome-wide median drop: dependent ≤ −1, partial −1 to −0.5. Boxes are the best TTGACA/TATAAT-like pair on the strand of the driven gene. They are putative, as is the TSS (−10 box + 7 nt). Conservation is identity across 4,064 plastid genera. TSS mapped is the nearest TAP-enriched 5′ end within 60 nt (Terminome-seq, Castandet et al. 2019). Literature sigma assignments are for A. thaliana; check them before citing. Sigma 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 cannot be assigned from these data.

Values are computed over each exact locus. The percentile compares the value with all 50-bp bins of the genome (100 = highest). Click a column to sort and a row to show it in the browser.

IPOD and ptChIP: sample/input coverage. ptATAC: Tn5 insertions, nucleoid/naked DNA. SOLINA: membrane/stroma coverage.
